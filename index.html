Common questions

Need more details? Contact us.

Is our data separate from other companies?

Yes. Each company account is isolated and can only be accessed by invited users.

Can we use it on a phone?

Yes. It opens in any modern browser and supports QR scanning through the phone camera.

Can we bring our existing Excel list?

Yes. Download the import template, adjust the columns, then upload. The system flags rows that need fixing.

Who can see and change what?

Super Admin, Staff and Viewer roles are available, with access permissions you can adjust.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your data stays safely stored and viewable, and can be edited again once your company package is activated.
